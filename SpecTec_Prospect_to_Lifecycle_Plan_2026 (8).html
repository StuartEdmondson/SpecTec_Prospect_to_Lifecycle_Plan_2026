Nurture and qualify Middle of funnel

Stage 4

Deal stage support Bottom of funnel

Conversion content — the primary commercial priority
Why we need thisSales has no structured content support during the deal cycle. No ROI asset, no migration reassurance, no executive summary for board sign-off. These are the assets most likely to accelerate deal velocity. The deal-stage HubSpot workflow is not yet built.
HubSpot: Deal-stage workflow to be built by Marketing. Triggered when Deal is created and contact associated. Content delivered via automated email sequence.
Implementation: three streams — same body of work, different buyer context

Stream A: No system to AMOS

Buyer has no current PMS. Content focuses on the case for structured asset management, the risk of doing nothing, and how AMOS structures the journey from zero to operational control.

Stream B: Competitor to AMOS

Buyer switching from Sertica, BASSnet, MariApps, or another platform. Content focuses on migration confidence, AMOS differentiation, and the proven transition methodology. Requires sign-off on competitive positioning.

Stream C: Legacy AMOS to new AMOS

Existing customer upgrading or moving to a new tier. Content focuses on what is new, investment protection, data continuity, and tier value. Joint work with Customer Success.

All three implementation streams are collaborative work between Marketing and Customer Success. Stream C also requires Product input on tier and version differentiation.

Stage 5

Customer onboarding Post-sale

Triggered at Closed-Won · Shared across all contacts
Why we need thisThe moment after Closed-Won is the highest-risk point for buyer regret. Without a structured onboarding sequence the customer's first post-sale experience is silence. None of these assets currently exist.
HubSpot: Closed-Won triggers lifecycle workflow. All associated contacts enrol in shared onboarding sequence. After sequence completes, workflow forks on Contact Category into Decision Maker pathway or User pathway.
Dependencies: Everything in this section must be built and agreed between Marketing, Commercial, and Customer Success before any workflow is configured.
